Service Agreement

Clear expectations for better care

A starter version that you can later replace with your final official agreement.

  1. Services are provided only after the requested scope and availability are confirmed.
  2. Families should provide accurate information relevant to the agreed care requirements.
  3. Caregivers should provide only services within their agreed role, training and service scope.
  4. Medical emergencies require immediate contact with emergency services or the treating doctor; JotnoSetu is not an emergency replacement service.
  5. Payment, service duration, cancellation and replacement terms should be confirmed before service begins.
  6. Both families and caregivers must maintain a respectful and safe environment.
  7. Patient privacy and dignity should be respected by everyone involved.
Launch note: Before public launch, your final agreement, privacy policy, liability wording and applicable local regulatory requirements should be reviewed by a qualified professional.
